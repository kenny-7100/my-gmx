# Solana trade math reference generator

Status: **compiled and accepted with Rust 1.90.0** on 2026-10-28. Cargo.lock and vectors.json retain the locked dependency graph and 223 model-generated reference cases. The previous 199 case inputs/outputs are unchanged. Two runs were byte-identical; verify.mjs independently checked all results, source/input/lock provenance, 79 malformed-input/setup failures and model errors distinct from zero. This tool is not a production dependency or a replacement for the shared GMX SDK.

The binary calls the real `gmsol-model` at the exact v0.10.0 commit. It accepts `inputs.json` and writes JSON to stdout only after all cases have been evaluated. The initial input set covers:

- `FeeParams::fee`: a supplied combined discount factor, fee balance direction, zero, full discount and rounding. It does not authenticate or compute GT rank/referral composition.
- `PerpMarketExt::cap_positive_position_price_impact`: configured positive cap and available impact pool, negative/zero passthrough. It does not calculate raw impact, distribute the pool, execute an order or calculate acceptable prices.
- `PositionExt::pnl_value`: long/short, both collateral types, side aggregate OI across both collaterals, profitable zero/finite/unreached caps, losses and partial-close rounding. The tool seeds explicit read-only mathematical state through model test builders; it does not validate or execute that position.
- `PositionExt::check_liquidatable`: 28 deposit/increase-initialized positions and 84 probes, each at three adjacent raw index price units around an analytically derived boundary. Covers long/short and both collateral types, market minimum collateral, leverage minima and their tie, nonpositive collateral, ordinary versus liquidation factors, explicit protocol `None` fallback, price spread, opening/closing fees and exclusion of liquidation fees from the predicate. Both liquidation and ordinary margin-check reasons are retained. No `PositionStatus.liquidation_price` is used.
- `IncreasePosition::execute`: 32 scenarios with positive/negative/zero linear impact and price spread across long/short and both collateral types. Covers uncapped positive impact, empty/insufficient/just-sufficient impact pool, the configured positive cap even when above the configured negative cap, worsening OI and cross-over OI. Each scenario retains a baseline action report and 3 independently supplied acceptable prices (96 probes): equality and the favorable adjacent price pass; the unfavorable adjacent price is rejected. Reports retain raw/capped impact, signed token impact, size/collateral, execution price, fees, funding outputs and the remaining impact pool.
- `PositionExt::check_liquidatable` with nonzero impact: 28 additional scenarios and 84 probes. Target positions open with real immediate negative impact; a separate opposite position is opened when needed to make the target's close worsen OI. Covers positive closing impact ignored, negative impact capped/uncapped/zero cap, minimum collateral, nonpositive collateral and ordinary margin boundaries. Both predicates use the liquidation impact cap; only their collateral factors differ. Opposite-action reports and actual side OI are independently verified. These fixed affine scenarios have zero fees, no price spread and no elapsed funding/borrowing; they do not establish a general liquidation-price solver.
- Borrowing accrual and settlement: 28 scenarios, 32 cumulative updates and 56 real subsequent increases. Covers both sides/collaterals, elapsed zero, nonzero USD fees truncating to zero collateral tokens, the first raw token unit, zero borrowing factor, split intervals and receiver shares of zero/37%/100%. Linear utilization uses the model's long-token reserve versus short USD reserve, with explicit oracle spreads. The first subsequent increase settles pending fees, credits liquidity/receiver pools and updates the position factor; a second increase at the same cumulative factor cannot charge those fees again. Funding updates are not invoked, so zero claimable amounts here do not validate funding settlement.
- Funding accrual and claimable reports: 16 scenarios with four real positions each, 18 funding updates and 128 subsequent increases. Both payer directions cover zero time/factor/cap, balanced OI, tiny rounding, ordinary elapsed time, rate caps and split intervals. Linear fallback rates are explicit; dynamic rate increases/decreases are disabled. Payer packed amounts and pending charges round up; receiver amounts round down. Receivers with either collateral can receive both collateral tokens. The next increase returns claimable amounts and advances all snapshots; a second increase without a funding update returns zero. Reports represent amounts the caller must transfer, not evidence of a vault transfer or wallet payout.

- Dynamic funding: 24 scenarios and 42 real updates, mirrored from both initial payer directions. Real increases change OI; stored rates are never injected. Covers growth, stability, both threshold equalities, decay to signed one, delayed direction reversal, rate caps, minimum active rates, zero time and balanced OI with an existing rate. Each checkpoint retains action reports, OI, signed stored rate, active rate and all four collateral/side deltas. This subset uses short collateral only and does not settle the existing positions.

- Funding distribution: 24 scenarios, 26 updates and 168 subsequent increases reuse the funding accrual/settlement implementation. Four independently supplied sizes cover asymmetric collateral OI, missing payer/receiver collateral positions and single-sided OI, mirrored across payer directions. Also covers split time, tiny fees, balanced OI, zero time/factor and capped rates. Absent positions are null; their market deltas remain explicit. Both claims can accrue to a receiver with only one collateral position. Single-sided fallback updates and subsequent settlement reports remain zero.

Amounts, USD20 values and factors are canonical integer strings. Prices are USD20 per smallest token unit. Token decimals are explicit in the original operations; dynamic funding uses the fixed tagged test defaults and distribution uses the documented fixed fixtures below. Fee output keeps the amount's input unit. Shared USD30 outputs append ten decimal places exactly, without i128 overflow. Model errors and `None` are distinct from real zero. Malformed inputs or setup failures abort the run; no partial reference set is accepted.

For the original positive-impact/PnL subset, only the fields read by each function are populated; unused fixed-tag `TestMarketConfig` defaults do not enter those read-only calculations. The liquidation subset executes the model's deposit and increase actions with explicit prices, liquidity amounts, collateral, size, minima and fee factors. Swap/position impact and swap fees are explicitly zero. Other action configuration (reserve/OI limits, PnL limits, borrowing/funding parameters and fixed-point adjustments) uses the exact tagged test model defaults; these are reproducible mathematical fixtures, not deployed market configuration. The model clock never advances, so accrued borrowing/funding is zero. These probes are loss-making and do not exercise the profitable PnL cap. Long-token prices follow index min only in the long-collateral scenarios; all three token price bounds are explicit in every probe. No RPC is executed.

The increase subset first deposits liquidity and opens a separate seed position with the real model. That action establishes OI and its token sizes; its report is retained. The input then supplies an explicit impact-pool snapshot, applied through the model pool delta API before the target action. This is controlled mathematical state for cap boundaries, not evidence of a pool funding transfer. Every baseline/acceptable-price attempt clones the same market and uses a fresh target position: a failed model action may mutate its trial state and is never reused. The diagnostic baseline omits the acceptable price only inside this offline tool; production market orders must still supply one. The impact exponent is exactly one, order/swap fees are zero, and no time advances or funding/borrowing update is executed. Min position/collateral are 1 USD, ordinary margin factor is 10%, and liquidation impact cap is 1%; remaining tagged test defaults are retained. Positive impact tokens truncate by index max; negative impact magnitude rounds up by index min. Long base tokens truncate by index max, short base tokens round up by index min, then signed impact changes tokens immediately. Execution price is the integer size/token ratio, not the oracle price or a formula using uncapped impact.

The long uncapped inputs use a larger long pool so the cap actually remains above side PnL even with the price spread. All 223 accepted cases have successful setup; the increase subset deliberately retains 32 expected limit-price rejections among its 96 probes. Separate adversarial runs check a real model conversion error and a liquidation-probe arithmetic error; errors never become a false liquidation result or zero. FeeParams::fee None is not reachable with canonical u128 inputs and factors constrained to [0, 10^20], so the defensive unavailable branch is not claimed as exercised. A liquidation factor must be present as a canonical string or explicit null; omission is rejected. Null exercises the tagged protocol's fallback, not a frontend fallback for a missing API field. The new `liquidation_impact` operation requires all its impact fields explicitly; the existing zero-impact operation retains its original behavior. SDK/API consumption and the remaining operations are subsequent work.

The borrowing subset explicitly initializes the borrowing clock, opens a position, advances the model clock by each supplied interval and executes `update_borrowing`. Pending position values use the stored cumulative factor; market projections include elapsed time. The tagged `TestMarket` clock also includes real wall time: every update must report exactly the requested interval, otherwise the generator aborts without JSON. Do not normalize a delayed run or remove the check. Intervals are nonempty and each is at most one year. Borrowing exponent is one, kink parameters are zero (disabled), and skipping the smaller side is false. Swap/order fees and impact are zero; other tagged test defaults remain. Subsequent increases have explicit nonzero size and collateral; they share one cumulative factor without another clock update. USD fees truncate to collateral tokens before receiver splitting. The position factor resets even when a nonzero USD fee truncates to zero tokens; no dust carry is invented. Pool conservation is verified in raw token units. These are mathematical fixtures, not live market settings or token transfers.

The funding subset uses equal position sizes within each side, one position per collateral. It initializes the funding clock, opens all four positions, advances explicit intervals and calls the real funding update. The same strict elapsed-time drift check applies. Funding is split by payer collateral OI, valued at collateral max prices and packed with the model's 10^10 adjustment. No borrowing update is invoked; order/swap fees and impacts are zero. All funding fields are explicit; both side sizes and subsequent increase size must be nonzero. Dynamic rate parameters are zero. Zero stored next-factor values in this fallback mode do not mean zero funding accrual. The verifier checks all report deltas, pending charges/claims, settlement snapshots and that total charges cover total claims per token; it does not simulate external vault transfers.

Dynamic funding uses exponent one, fallback factor zero and explicit increase/decrease/min/max/threshold factors. It deposits 100000000000000 raw long and 10000000000000 raw short tokens, then opens each added position with 1000000000 raw short collateral. Both OI sides must remain nonzero. Fees and impacts are zero; no borrowing update occurs. Other tagged defaults and the strict elapsed-time drift rejection remain. Growth uses the skew ratio rounded before multiplication; decay preserves signed one when its decrement reaches the stored magnitude. The stored rate is capped by max; min bounds only the active rate and maps zero to positive min. Balanced OI can retain a prior dynamic rate. Short-collateral payer deltas use two ceilings, receiver deltas two floors; long-collateral deltas are zero. These checkpoints do not verify settlement after a direction reversal or external token transfers.

Funding distribution orders its four sizes as (long,long collateral), (long,short collateral), (short,long collateral), (short,short collateral). Sizes may be zero but cannot all be zero. Decimals are 9/9/6; liquidity deposits are 100000000000000 long and 10000000000000 short raw units. Each active position receives 10000000000 long or 1000000000 short raw collateral. Each later increase adds 1 USD and 1000000000 long or 100000000 short raw collateral. These fixed mathematical fixtures are not deployed settings. Rates use the linear fallback; dynamic parameters are zero. Collateral funding values are independently floored from each payer collateral's OI share, then payer packing uses that collateral OI while receiver packing uses the full receiver side. No borrowing update, fees, impact or external transfer occurs. A zero side returns an empty funding report before rate calculation. This subset starts with a zero stored factor and does not verify transition from an existing dynamic rate to single-sided OI.
